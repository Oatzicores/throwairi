# ThrowAirI — Domain Glossary

Terms as used in this project. Definitions only; no implementation detail.

**Creator (ครีเอเตอร์)**
The site's primary visitor: a Thai person who makes video, image, audio or written content and is choosing AI tools for that work.

**Trust phase (ช่วงสร้างความน่าเชื่อถือ)**
The period before any Affiliate link or advertisement is live. The site earns nothing; its job is to prove its data can be relied on.

**Catalog (แคตตาล็อก)**
The full set of AI tools the site knows about. Every fact in it must be traceable to a page someone actually opened.

**Tool (เครื่องมือ AI)**
One entry in the Catalog: an AI product a visitor might choose, with its pricing, capabilities, reviews and usage signals.

**Sourced fact**
A single claim about a Tool, stored together with how sure we are (confidence), the page it came from (source URL) and the day it was last checked (last verified). A claim with no source is not stated as fact; it is recorded as *unknown*.

**Ranking (อันดับ)**
The order in which matching Tools are shown for a visitor's request. It is computed only from the Tool's own data and the visitor's request. No commercial relationship (affiliate, sponsorship, payment) may change it.

**Affiliate link**
An outbound link to a Tool's website that pays the site a commission when a visitor buys a paid plan. It is always disclosed to the visitor and never affects Ranking.

**Trend idea (ไอเดียจากเทรนด์)**
A suggested request shown on the home form, written by us from a published statistic about how people use AI. It is not a real search that visitors typed, and the site must not describe it as one.

**Commercial-use rights (สิทธิ์เชิงพาณิชย์)**
Whether a Tool's plan lets a customer use its output for business purposes. Tracked per plan as a Sourced fact.
